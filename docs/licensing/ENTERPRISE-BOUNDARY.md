# Formbricks Enterprise Boundary

Formbricks Enterprise functionality must be treated separately from the AGPL core.

Audit examples include SSO/SAML/OIDC enterprise workflows, teams and advanced roles, audit logs, two-factor authentication, contacts, quotas, workflows, advanced analysis, whitelabel features, and billing/cloud-specific functionality.

These names are an audit starting point, not a complete legal classification.

## Rule
Do not copy Enterprise source into Tallynest without explicit rights.

For each Enterprise capability choose:
1. independent Tallynest implementation;
2. compatible open-source implementation;
3. licensed commercial implementation/rights;
4. intentional omission.

Record the decision in the parity and decision documents.
